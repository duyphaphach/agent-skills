# Wording rules - English (en)

- Default to plain, spoken English. Prefer the common word over the formal one (use "buy" not "purchase", "help" not "facilitate").
- Pick one spelling standard per article - US or UK - and hold it. Match the target market.
- Second person ("you") for how-to and guide content.
- Contractions are fine ("you'll", "it's"). They read as natural, not sloppy.
- Numbers: numerals for 10 and above, words for one to nine, unless a unit follows.
- Date: US market `Month DD, YYYY`; UK market `DD Month YYYY`.
- Currency: `$1,234.56` style for US, `£1,234.56` for UK.
- Gloss a technical term inline the first time it appears.
