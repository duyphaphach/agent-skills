# Wording rules - Vietnamese (vi)

- Write natural, modern Vietnamese. Avoid stiff, translated-sounding phrasing.
- Keep widely-known English technical terms in English when the Vietnamese form is rare or awkward (e.g. "marketing", "email"). Gloss the rest in Vietnamese.
- Use correct Vietnamese diacritics everywhere. Never publish un-accented text.
- Address the reader as "bạn" for how-to and guide content.
- Numbers: thousands separator is a dot, decimal mark is a comma (`1.234,56`).
- Currency: `1.234.000 đ` or `1.234.000 VND`.
- Date: `DD/MM/YYYY`.
- Keep sentences short. Vietnamese runs long when translated literally from English. Break it up.
