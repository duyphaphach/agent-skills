# Wording rules - common across locales

Applies to every locale. A specific locale file (`en.md`, `vi.md`, ...) can override these.

- Write in the reader's language. Do not mix languages in a sentence unless the term has no local equivalent.
- Keep a brand or product name in its original form. Do not translate it.
- Localize examples: currency, dates, units, names, and places match the target market.
- Match the locale's number format (decimal mark, thousands separator).
- Match the locale's date format.
- Spell out a term the first time, then use the short form.
