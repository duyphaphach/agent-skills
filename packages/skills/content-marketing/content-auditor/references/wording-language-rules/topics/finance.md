# Wording rules - Finance

Finance content carries real consequences for the reader. Hold a higher bar.

## Required

- State that the article is information, not personal financial advice.
- Source every rate, return, fee, and statistic. Date it - finance numbers go stale fast.
- Name the risk next to any potential gain. Never show upside alone.
- Cite the regulator or central bank for the locale when rules are involved.

## Forbidden

- No guaranteed returns, "risk-free", or "get rich" framing.
- No specific buy or sell calls on a named asset.
- No urgency or fear pressure ("act now", "before it is too late").

## Tone

- Calm, precise, neutral. Let the numbers carry the weight.
- Define every term on first use - APR, yield, liquidity, diversification.
- Spell out assumptions behind any projection or example.
