# Wording rules - common across topics

Applies to every topic. A specific topic file (`finance.md`, ...) can override these.

- Define the core term of the topic early, in one plain sentence.
- Lead with what the reader gets, then the detail.
- One worked example beats three abstract explanations.
- Match claims to evidence. A strong claim needs a strong source.
- Name the limits. If advice does not fit every reader, say who it does not fit.
- Keep jargon to what the audience already knows. Gloss the rest.
