# Sources

Where `content-researcher` looks for articles. Edit this list freely - add the publications your team trusts, remove ones you do not.

The researcher gathers 20 articles per run, drawing across these sources.

## Search engines (start here)

- Google
- Bing
- DuckDuckGo

Use the keyword scoped to the location (region and language) for local-accurate results.

## Quality content sources

General publications known for well-researched, well-structured articles:

- Medium
- Substack (established newsletters)
- Industry trade publications for the topic at hand

## Topic-specific sources

Add the sources that matter for your niches. Examples:

| Topic | Sources |
|-------|---------|
| Finance | Investopedia, NerdWallet, the central bank or regulator site for the locale |
| Marketing | HubSpot blog, Content Marketing Institute, Ahrefs blog |
| Technology | official product docs, established engineering blogs |

## Rules

- Prefer the original source over an aggregator or a reprint.
- Skip thin affiliate pages and content-farm articles - they fail the rating in step 3 anyway.
- For a fast-moving topic, weight recent sources higher.
